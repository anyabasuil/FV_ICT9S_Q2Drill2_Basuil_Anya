About Our School

Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center is a progressive co-educational school in the Philippines that follows the educational philosophy of Dr. Maria Montessori.

The school aims to develop independent, responsible, and productive individuals. Students are encouraged to explore their interests, develop their abilities, and become lifelong learners.
